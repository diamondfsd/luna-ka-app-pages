PRIVACY

隐私说明

Luna咔不需要账号，设备设置和连接信息主要用于完成本机相机控制与素材管理。

设备权限

相机、麦克风、蓝牙、本地网络、动作与照片权限只会在对应功能需要时请求。用户拒绝权限后，相关功能可能不可用，但不影响未依赖该权限的其他功能。

Device permissions

Camera, microphone, Bluetooth, local network, motion, and photo permissions are requested when the related feature needs them. Declining a permission may disable that feature without affecting unrelated features.

本地数据

应用偏好、连接端点、创作预设和素材记录可能保存在设备本地。删除应用或清除数据可能同时删除这些本地记录。

Local data

App preferences, connection endpoints, creative presets, and media records may be stored on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may also remove those local records.

匿名使用统计

正式版可能发送匿名产品事件，用于了解启动、页面访问和核心功能是否正常工作。事件可包含随机设备标识、App 版本、系统名称与版本；不启用用户画像，并关闭基于 IP 的地理信息处理。

Anonymous usage events

Release builds may send anonymous product events to understand app startup, page visits, and core feature reliability. Events may include a random device identifier, app version, and OS name and version. Person profiles are disabled, and IP-based geolocation processing is turned off.

媒体与第三方服务

相机中的照片和视频仅在用户主动浏览、下载或导出时处理。若用户配置并使用第三方 AI、语音或其他外部服务，相关内容会按照该服务的规则处理，请先阅读对应服务的隐私政策。

Media and third-party services

Photos and videos on the camera are processed only when you browse, download, or export them. If you configure a third-party AI, speech, or other external service, related content is handled under that provider's terms; review its privacy policy first.
